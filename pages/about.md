### Hi, I’m Marcos Barbero—Welcome to My World Through the Lens

By profession, I’m a technology leader, working at the intersection of engineering and innovation. But beyond the world of code, strategy, and leadership, photography has been my creative escape—my way of slowing down and seeing the world with a different perspective.

<br />
I first picked up a camera out of curiosity, drawn to the challenge of capturing fleeting moments in a way that felt meaningful. Over time, what started as a hobby turned into a true passion. Whether it’s the intricate details of a cityscape, the raw emotion of a portrait, or the serenity of nature, I love the process of finding beauty in the everyday.

<br />
This portfolio is a reflection of that journey—a collection of the places I’ve been, the emotions I’ve experienced, and the stories I want to share. I hope my images speak to you, just as much as they do to me.
